# A birthday surprise 💗

## Editing the content
Everything personal lives in **`src/data/content.ts`**: her name, your name, the password (your special date), the "how we met" lines, the love letter, the reasons jar, captions and the finale lines.

## Photos
Put your photos in `public/photos/`:
- `couple1.jpg` — main photo (required)
- `her1.jpg` — optional solo photo of her. Delete it to skip that screen.

Keep faces in frame with `objectPosition` in `content.ts` (e.g. `"50% 30%"`). Switch the photo screen between `"polaroid"` and `"strip"` with `photoStyle`.

## Sharing it
Click **Publish** in Lovable to get a link you can send her.
